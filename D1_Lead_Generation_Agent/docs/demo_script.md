# 3-minute D1 Demo Script

0:00–0:20 — Problem
“Sales teams spend time finding and qualifying prospects manually. Our agent automates the repetitive discovery-to-qualification workflow.”

0:20–0:40 — ICP
Enter:
B2B SaaS | India | 50–500 employees | Hiring/Growth | Founder, CEO, CTO, Head of Security

0:40–1:10 — Discovery
Click Generate Leads. Show the pipeline and public source URLs.

1:10–1:30 — Compliance
Open the robots.txt/compliance log. Explain that every page fetch is gated by `urllib.robotparser`; disallowed or unverifiable pages are skipped.

1:30–1:55 — Enrichment
Open a lead and show industry, size, technology, hiring/growth and source evidence.

1:55–2:25 — Dedup + scoring
Show duplicate count and a lead score. Expand the reasoning and category points.

2:25–2:45 — Export
Download the normal CSV and HubSpot-style CSV.

2:45–3:00 — Refresh
Run again and explain caching/rate-limit handling and the re-runnable workflow.
